Songwriting Order

spoiler: there is no one true order of operations (however...)

When training/practicing/drilling or when "not inspired" - start with one given aspect and generate the rest from it - and yes, this part will be boring, but that is just the drilling and practicing. Do that starting from every aspect to all the others.

When following inspiration, you have ways of getting around stuck points.

The essence of music is melody. Especially if one considers groove, riffs, and basslines as melody. Melody is not justa selection or sequence of pitches - it's a sequence of sounds in time. Melody is sound and rhythm - pitch and rhythm

Harmony is texture and relationships between simultaneous melodies

For the above graph - the red dashed lines represent "informing" or "keeping in mind", while the solid line represents derivation paths and methods that can be practiced.

i.e. Always keep the concept and title in mind when doing each part - when writing a real song.

When practicing an isolated skill - you'd focus just on the "solid line" bit.

The title to lyrics line is in reference to writing chorus lyrics (or refrain if writing a Dylan style AAA song)

Solid line bits

  • Lyrics → Melody
  • Lyrics ← Melody
  • Harmony → Melody
  • Harmony ← Melody
  • Harmony → Groove
  • Harmony ← Groove
